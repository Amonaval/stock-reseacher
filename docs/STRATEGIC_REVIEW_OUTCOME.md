# Strategic Review Outcome

This review follows the research-foundation consolidation, background-job hardening, deep-gap research and Bull/Bear challenge work.

## Product decision

Continue the product.

The strongest product thesis remains:

> **Personal AI Equity Research Operating System — autonomous by default, evidence-driven, investor-controlled and self-challenging.**

The product should not compete by displaying more ratios or offering generic AI chat over filings. Those capabilities are already widely available.

The differentiated loop is the combination of:

1. learn or import the investor's historical screening philosophy;
2. convert that philosophy into editable, reusable strategies;
3. execute screening autonomously;
4. preserve exact identity and source provenance;
5. let the investor review/override every expensive stage;
6. collect financial history automatically;
7. build source-linked company dossiers;
8. expose unknowns and failed source acquisition;
9. actively resolve research gaps;
10. independently construct Bull and Bear cases;
11. measure confidence in the research itself before valuation;
12. later connect evidence to valuation, conviction and portfolio construction;
13. eventually maintain a living research memory over time.

## What is commodity vs differentiated

### Commodity / required overlap

Approximately 30–35% of the product will naturally overlap with existing research tools:

- screeners;
- financial statements and ratios;
- filings and annual reports;
- transcripts / presentations;
- valuation data;
- basic alerts and watchlists.

These are necessary inputs, not the moat.

### Differentiated core

The strongest differentiated workflows are:

- **Investor-philosophy learning → editable executable strategy profile**;
- **Progressive autonomous research with visible evidence gaps and operator gates**;
- **Persistent source-linked dossier + independent Bear challenge + research-confidence quality control**.

Later, management promise-vs-delivery memory can become another strong differentiator.

## Strategic decisions

### 1. Research confidence is separate from conviction

A company can have:

- high-quality research and a bearish thesis;
- low-quality research and a very bullish-looking narrative;
- a contested thesis supported by excellent evidence.

Therefore the product must never use research-confidence scores as stock-attractiveness scores.

### 2. Evidence quality must precede valuation

Valuation without reliable business/financial context creates false precision.

Before valuation, the product should understand:

- source authority;
- source freshness;
- evidence traceability;
- fundamental-research mission coverage;
- cross-source corroboration;
- downside/governance evidence;
- contradictions;
- unresolved critical questions;
- financial-data coverage.

### 3. Do not require every possible source class

A company should not be blocked simply because it lacks a transcript or credit-rating report.

Research readiness should depend on sufficient authoritative longitudinal + current evidence and fundamental mission coverage, while optional missing source classes remain visible.

### 4. The next permanent data architecture should be provider-based

Screener remains a POC adapter.

Future first-class adapters should separate:

- security identity/master data;
- financial statements;
- prices/market data;
- exchange filings;
- company investor-relations documents;
- ratings;
- transcripts;
- industry/news context.

NSE/BSE should eventually become authoritative identity/filing sources where practical.

### 5. Do not add arbitrary scores when an explicit reason is better

The default investor experience remains:

```text
Decision
→ Why
→ Evidence
→ Missing information
→ Next action
```

Numeric scores are secondary diagnostics and prioritization aids.

### 6. Sector-aware financial/valuation models are required before serious valuation

Banks, NBFCs, insurers, utilities, commodity businesses and normal operating companies should not share one generic valuation model.

The valuation layer should therefore be pluggable and sector/business-model aware rather than one universal formula.

## Current next mission

**Evidence Confidence + Research Dossier v2**

The new quality-control layer evaluates research process quality rather than investment attractiveness.

Dimensions:

- source authority;
- source freshness;
- evidence traceability;
- fundamental mission coverage;
- cross-source triangulation;
- downside/governance evidence coverage;
- contradiction handling;
- financial-data coverage;
- unresolved-question penalty.

Outputs:

- `HIGH_RESEARCH_CONFIDENCE`
- `MODERATE_RESEARCH_CONFIDENCE`
- `LOW_RESEARCH_CONFIDENCE`
- explicit critical gaps;
- `READY_FOR_VALUATION_CONTEXT` or `MORE_RESEARCH_NEEDED`.

`READY_FOR_VALUATION_CONTEXT` means only that the research foundation is strong enough to support valuation work. It does not mean undervalued, attractive or BUY.

## Sequence after this mission

If the evidence-confidence layer proves useful on real companies:

1. **Valuation Intelligence v1** — sector-aware, scenario/range based, provenance visible;
2. **Conviction Engine** — combine thesis quality, evidence confidence, valuation asymmetry and risks without collapsing everything into one opaque number;
3. **Final comparison/ranking** — explain why one researched opportunity ranks above another;
4. **Portfolio construction** — risk-aware capital allocation, not automatic trading;
5. **Continuous monitoring** — detect evidence/thesis changes over time;
6. **Learning engine** — evaluate which strategy/research signals actually helped historically.

## Product refusal list

The product should refuse to become:

- a black-box stock-tip generator;
- an automated trading engine;
- a collection of unexplained scores;
- an AI system that hallucinates missing facts;
- a funnel that advances weak-evidence companies just to fill quotas;
- a system that treats management guidance as fact;
- a system that infers durable competitive advantage from one ratio;
- a feature-count race against established terminals.

## Success measure

The research system is successful if a serious investor can reach a better-supported conclusion with materially less manual work **while still understanding and controlling the reasoning path**.

## Known major gap

The system still does not fully execute the V2 master strategies over a complete live Indian equity universe by itself. Today, Screener result export/import remains a convenient path into the candidate universe.

Closing this is the next major autonomy milestone: **Autonomous Market Data & Screening Gateway**.

## Current maturity

This should be treated as an advanced prototype/research platform. It is not yet a completed investment decision system.
